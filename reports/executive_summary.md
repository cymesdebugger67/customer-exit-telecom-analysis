# Executive Summary

## The Customer Exit - Telecom Analytics

The analysis covers **7,043 customers**.
A total of **1,869 customers churned**,
producing an overall churn rate of **26.54%**.

The largest descriptive churn signals include:

- **Month-to-month** contract churn:
  **42.71%**
- **0-6 months** tenure churn:
  **52.94%**
- **Fiber optic** internet-service churn:
  **41.89%**
- **Electronic check** payment-method churn:
  **45.29%**

The highest-risk sufficiently large combined profile is:

**Month-to-month / 0-6 months /
Fiber optic**

with **74.15% churn**
across **619 customers**.

The strongest categorical association in the tested feature set is
**protection_level** with Cramer's V =
**0.412**.

The strongest tested numeric separation is **tenure**
with absolute rank-biserial effect =
**0.480**.

These findings are descriptive and observational and do not establish
causation.
