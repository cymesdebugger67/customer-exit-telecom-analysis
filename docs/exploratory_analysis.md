# Exploratory Analysis

## Purpose

Exploratory data analysis is used to understand the structure of the telecom customer population before formal statistical analysis.

## Customer Lifecycle

Customer tenure ranges from 0 to 72 months.

The highest observed tenure-group churn occurs among customers with 0–6 months of tenure.

Observed churn progressively declines across longer-tenure groups.

## Contract Structure

Month-to-month customers show substantially higher observed churn than customers with one-year or two-year contracts.

This segment is important because it combines elevated churn with large customer volume.

## Internet Service

Fiber optic customers show higher observed churn than DSL customers and customers without internet service.

This relationship is interpreted together with contract type, tenure, billing characteristics, and support services rather than as an independent causal explanation.

## Payment Behavior

Electronic-check customers show substantially higher observed churn than the other payment-method groups.

Automatic payment methods show lower observed churn in this sample.

## Monthly Charges

Monthly charges differ meaningfully between churned and retained customers.

However, churn does not increase perfectly with monthly price.

The 70–99.99 monthly-charge band currently has the highest observed charge-band churn rate.

## Support and Security

Customers without technical support or online security show elevated observed churn.

The project also creates derived support and protection profiles for multidimensional analysis.

These engineered features are analytical groupings rather than independent causal drivers.

## Multidimensional Analysis

Important combinations include:

- contract × tenure
- internet service × contract
- payment method × paperless billing
- support × online security
- contract × tenure × internet service

The combined analysis identifies customer profiles where elevated churn and meaningful customer volume overlap.
